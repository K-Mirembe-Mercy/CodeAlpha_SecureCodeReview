# Contributing

Suggestions are welcome, especially:

- Flaws in `app_secure.py` that were missed or only partly fixed
- Corrections to OWASP or CWE mappings in `REVIEW_REPORT.md`
- Clearer explanations or remediation advice

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repo and create a branch: `git checkout -b fix/short-description`
2. Make your change and keep it focused.
3. Open a pull request describing what changed and why.

## Ground rules

- Do not add real credentials, personal data, or working exploits against third-party systems.
- New vulnerable examples must be clearly marked as intentional.
- Be respectful and constructive.
